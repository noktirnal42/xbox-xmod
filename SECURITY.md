# Security Policy

## Scope

This repository documents security research on operator-owned Xbox consoles in a home lab. It is not a product with vulnerabilities of its own; there is nothing here to "report a bug in" in the traditional sense.

## If you believe content here is harmful

- If you are a Microsoft employee and believe something here crosses a line, open an issue or contact the repo owner — we will discuss and remove as needed, consistent with the [Xbox One Research](https://xboxoneresearch.github.io/) community's stated approach of working with Microsoft.
- If you find personal data, credentials, or anything accidentally committed, open an issue immediately; it will be scrubbed and history rewritten.

## What this project will not do

- No piracy tooling, no copyright-circumvention for playing unlicensed content.
- No targeting of consoles, accounts, or services belonging to anyone but the lab operator.
- No interaction with Xbox Live beyond unauthenticated public endpoints.
